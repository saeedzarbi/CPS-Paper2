## New arms
- `tube_v2_step_split_aX` — split objective: rebuffer penalty in the MPC objective uses the point (harmonic) forecast; the buffer-safety constraint uses the conformal lower bound. Static 1-step cap kept.
- `tube_v2_step_static_aX` / `tube_v2_step_split_aX`, α ∈ {0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3} (α also drives the static cap).
- `tube_v2_maxscore_aX` (α ∈ {0.1, 0.2}) — simultaneous joint tube: S_j = max_h s_{j,h}/σ_{j,h} (σ = per-step (1-α) score quantile frozen at decision time, floor 0.02), one quantile at 1-α; pre-update bounds; joint flag per completed decision.

★ = QoE–rebuffer Pareto-efficient within the trace set.

## Findings
1. **α is the main lever.** `step_static_a0.15` vs `robust_mpc_logu`: 5g_harsh ΔQoE +5.67 [+2.47,+9.01], Δrebuf −1.41 [−2.15,−0.66], ΔVMAF −0.16 (ns); bb ≈ tie; 5g_markov ΔQoE −5.46 (ns), Δrebuf +1.56 (borderline). Per-step coverage ≈ 0.85 = 1−α as designed.
2. **Split objective hurts.** Log-utility does not depend on throughput, so the split only makes predicted rebuffer optimistic → more stalls (5g_markov Δrebuf +9.7 s at α=0.1). Rejected.
3. **Max-score tube delivers the joint guarantee.** α=0.1 → joint 0.89–0.90, α=0.2 → 0.80–0.81 (on target), dominating Bonferroni (Phase 2 joint 0.93–0.95) on QoE at similar joint coverage (5g_harsh 49.7 vs 46.3; bb 33.1 vs 25.6), but it remains conservative vs MPC baselines.
4. ACI (γ=0.002) ≈ static, as in Phase 2.

## Known caveat
Per-step scores / bounds follow the v2 indexing convention (decision j's step-h bound is scored against chunk j+h, while the planner uses step 1 for the chunk being downloaded). This is internally consistent (calibrated quantity = reported coverage) but is one step more conservative than necessary; audit before final test.
