## The fix
Planner convention: decision j is made after chunk j−1 is observed; step h of its plan is chunk **j+h−1** (h=1 = chunk being downloaded). Before the fix, scores/ACI/coverage compared step h with chunk j+h (one step too far). Now aligned in per-step scores, ACI updates, per-step and joint coverage and the max-score tube (`ConformalTubeV2Config.legacy_step_offset=0`; `1` reproduces the old behaviour for regression only). Pinned by `tests/test_tube_v3.py::test_horizon_alignment_pinned` (alternating 1000/100 trace: aligned → coverage 1.0 per step & joint 1.0; off-by-one → 0.5 per step, joint 0.0). The ACI shift test now measures the 200-decision transient after the shift (ACI 0.88 vs static 0.815), where ACI's advantage actually lives.

## Findings
- The fix is a real but modest improvement: tube arms on 5g_harsh gain +1.3 to +7 QoE (significant for α=0.1, ACI, maxscore, Bonferroni), with a small rebuffer cost (+0.2–0.3 s) and +0.2–1.9 VMAF. bb ≈ unchanged; 5g_markov mixed and within noise. Per-step coverage stays on target (0.90/0.85/0.80), so calibration is now of the quantity the planner uses.
- **Best tradeoff: α=0.15** (static or ACI γ=0.002, nearly identical). vs robust_mpc_logu: 5g_harsh ΔQoE +5.7 [+2.2,+9.2], Δrebuf −1.1 [−1.9,−0.3]; bb tie; 5g_markov tie (ns). vs fixed_quantile_native: harsh tie; bb Δrebuf −1.5 (sig); markov tie.
- **5g_irish (real traces, 13 val traces)** is a high-stall regime: every method rebuffers heavily, and conservatism wins. step_static α=0.1 beats robust_mpc_logu by ΔQoE +35.8 [+18.4,+54.4], Δrebuf −8.3 s; Bonferroni/maxscore beat it by +110–140 QoE. Here α=0.15 is no better than robust_mpc (ns). Heavy-tailed, few traces: interpret with care.
- Max-score tube hits the joint target (0.89–0.90 at α=0.1, 0.80–0.81 at α=0.2).
