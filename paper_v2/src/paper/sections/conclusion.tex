\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented Tube-MPC with a max-score conformal throughput tube, pre-refill safe planning, and chunk-aligned frozen calibration.
On \mbox{5G} test suites the method reduces rebuffering versus RobustMPC while improving QoE on Markov and real Irish traces, with empirical joint coverage near nominal levels.
Broadband remains challenging: outage-heavy traces limit all controllers, and joint coverage requires aligned calibration and honest reporting when nominal targets are not met.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Exchangeability and fixed-\(\sigma\) assumptions hold only approximately; the executed policy includes a one-step conformal cap; per-family \(\alpha\) is a validated hyperparameter; effective throughput depends weakly on the calibration reference policy.
