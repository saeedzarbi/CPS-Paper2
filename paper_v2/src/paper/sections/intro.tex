\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Client-side Adaptive Bitrate (ABR) controllers must balance video quality against rebuffering under volatile wireless throughput.
Model Predictive Control (MPC) plans several chunks ahead using a point forecast of future capacity, but a single mean forecast hides tail risk: a conservative plan wastes quality, while an aggressive plan stalls playback when the link dips.

Conformal prediction offers distribution-free uncertainty sets from exchangeable calibration scores~\cite{vovk2005algorithmic,angelopoulos2023gentle}.
Recent ABR work applies one-step conformal shields or risk budgets~\cite{zhong2026conformalabr,liu2025safesabr}.
\textbf{Paper~1} in this line introduced a one-step Certified Perceptual Shield (CPS) with a conformal throughput lower bound and VMAF-knee banking.
\textbf{This paper} extends uncertainty to a \emph{multi-step tube}: at each decision we publish lower bounds on throughput for the next \(H\) chunks and plan a monotone bitrate trajectory inside that tube.

We make three design choices that distinguish the system from a naive stack of \(H\) independent conformal intervals.
First, a \emph{max-score simultaneous} conformal tube controls the event that \emph{all} \(H\) horizon steps are covered at once, with scales \(\sigma_h\) and a single joint quantile \(\widehat Q\) (Section~\ref{sec:method:maxscore}).
Second, a \emph{pre-refill safety} constraint in Tube-MPC rejects plans that would drain the buffer on the conformal lower-bound trajectory (Section~\ref{sec:method:mpc}).
Third, \emph{frozen split calibration} (\texttt{\_fs}) fits \(\sigma\) and \(\widehat Q\) on disjoint calibration traces and replays chunk-level effective throughput through the same simulator used at test time, aligning offline scores with runtime observations (Section~\ref{sec:method:calib}).

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Tube-MPC with conformal tubes.} Finite-horizon MPC over a multi-step conformal lower bound on throughput, composed with a one-step static conformal cap on the executed action.
  \item \textbf{Max-score joint tube + safety lemma.} A simultaneous coverage statement under frozen scales (Theorem~\ref{thm:maxscore}) and a conditional no-rebuffer lemma for the safe planner (Lemma~\ref{lem:safety}).
  \item \textbf{Leakage-free evaluation.} Pre-registered per-family \(\alpha\) selection on validation only, single frozen test run, trace-cluster bootstrap CIs, and manifest-verified traces.
  \item \textbf{Empirical study.} On synthetic and real \mbox{5G} suites the primary tube arm cuts rebuffering versus RobustMPC with large QoE gains on Markov and Irish traces; broadband joint coverage improves after chunk-aligned calibration but remains a limitation (Section~\ref{sec:eval}).
\end{itemize}
