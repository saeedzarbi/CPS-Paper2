\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{ABR and MPC.}
Classical controllers include BBA~\cite{huang2014buffer}, BOLA~\cite{spiteri2018bola}, and RobustMPC~\cite{yin2015control}.
MPC variants forecast throughput and optimize a QoE objective over a short horizon; Kairos~\cite{zhong2025kairos} conditions uncertainty on buffer state but does not provide conformal certificates.

\paragraph{Conformal methods in streaming.}
Conformal-ABR~\cite{zhong2026conformalabr} wraps predictors with split-conformal intervals under an explicit stall-risk budget.
SafeSABR~\cite{liu2025safesabr} combines learning with runtime auditing.
Our work differs by planning over \(H\) steps with a \emph{simultaneous} tube and an explicit safe MPC layer, rather than a single-step projection only.

\paragraph{Multi-step conformal prediction.}
Weighted and max-score constructions appear in the conformal forecasting literature~\cite{cleaveland2024weighted}; we apply a max-score tube to throughput residuals aligned with chunk indices and cite Cleaveland et al.\ for the construction while focusing on the ABR integration and evaluation protocol.

\paragraph{Relation to Paper~1 (CPS).}
The one-step CPS shield guarantees per-chunk coverage of the next throughput sample.
Tube-MPC uses multi-step lower bounds for planning; the executed action is still capped by the same one-step conformal projector for buffer feasibility.
